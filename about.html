The mangal

Cooked over real charcoal

A mangal is the traditional Turkish charcoal grill, and it's the heart of our kitchen.

Our lamb and chicken are marinated in-house, threaded onto skewers and cooked to order over charcoal. That gives every shish, adana and lamb chop its smoky, chargrilled flavour. Mains come with rice and a fresh salad of tomato, red onion, sumac and herbs.

Alongside the grill you'll find homemade meze, house specialities like Iskender and Sarma Beyti, fresh fish, and plenty of vegetarian dishes.

Welcome

Generous, warm hospitality

We're a family business, and we want every guest to feel at home. Sit down and we'll bring you warm bread, dips and our chilli sauce to start, then serve generous plates from the grill.

Whether it's a quick weeknight dinner, a family meal with the kids or a long evening of meze and wine with friends, you'll find a table at Villa Mangal.
